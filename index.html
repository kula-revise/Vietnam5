Beyond the exam

You just did the thing an essay is actually for: you took a pile of things you knew and turned them into a case, a clear line held from first sentence to last, tested against the other side, and judged. That is not a History trick. It is how you win an argument that matters, in a job, in a disagreement, in your own head.

Facts are cheap and everywhere. The person who can take them and build a fair, honest, convincing argument is the one who gets listened to. That is the whole point of learning to do this.
